GenAI Insights is a data analytics project focused on exploring Generative AI adoption, workforce impact, productivity changes and industry-level trends through interactive visualizations.

About GenAI Insights

Generative Artificial Intelligence is becoming an important part of business operations. Understanding how organizations adopt these tools and how their use relates to workforce and productivity indicators can help people explore emerging technology trends.

GenAI Insights brings these topics together in one interactive analytics platform. It uses data visualization to make patterns across AI tools, industries, countries and adoption years easier to explore.

The project demonstrates how data analytics technologies can turn a dataset into meaningful visual summaries and support data-driven exploration.
