ABOUT THE PROJECT

Turning GenAI Data Into Meaningful Insights

GenAI Insights is a data analytics project focused on understanding Generative AI adoption and its reported workforce and productivity outcomes.

The project examines adoption patterns across industries, countries and AI tools. Interactive visualizations help users explore the dataset and compare different aspects of AI adoption.

Results describe patterns in the available dataset and should not automatically be interpreted as proof that AI caused a particular outcome.
